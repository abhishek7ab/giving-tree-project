🛡️ Platform Instructions & Community Guidelines

How Giving Tree Works & Our Platform Role

Giving Tree is a peer-to-peer neighborhood bridge connecting donors and receivers directly across Pune.

Direct P2P Bridge (Like OLX)

Giving Tree operates as a 100% non-commercial peer-to-peer connection service. We connect verified Pune neighbors directly without middleman warehousing or courier markups. Donors and receivers agree on handovers with full mutual autonomy.

Direct Neighbor-to-Neighbor Handover

Fair-Share 5-Item Quota

To prevent hoarding and guarantee equal opportunity for everyone in Pune, each member can hold up to 5 active requests concurrently.

Anti-Hoarding Protection

10 Pune Landmark Hubs

Handovers are pinned to familiar public landmarks (Kothrud, Baner, FC Road, Hinjawadi, Viman Nagar) for safe, daylight meetups.

Public Daylight Meetups Only

Secure 4-Digit Handover Verification & Zero-Tolerance Policy

Every item transfer uses a cryptographic 4-digit PIN confirmation upon physical handover. Any commercial resale, deceptive listing, or no-show behavior triggers immediate investigation and permanent account suspension.
