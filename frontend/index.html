📜 Platform Instructions & Community Guidelines

How Giving Tree Connects You (P2P Model)

Please review our community rules, fair-share request limits, and public meetup instructions before requesting or donating items.

OLX-Style P2P Facilitator

Giving Tree acts as a pure direct connection platform (like OLX for 100% free donations). We do not ship or warehouse physical goods—we provide the trusted technology for Pune neighbors to connect and give items directly.

Fair-Share 5-Item Request Quota

To ensure fair distribution and prevent hoarding, each member is allowed a maximum of 5 active requests at a time. Once a handover is completed or closed, new requests can be made.

10 Pune Public Neighborhood Hubs

Pickups take place at designated public neighborhood landmarks across 10 Pune hubs (Kothrud, Baner, FC Road, Hinjawadi, Viman Nagar, etc.) to keep meetups safe, convenient, and transparent.

Safe Handovers & Community Accountability

Always meet in well-lit public spots. The platform facilitates direct handovers and provides instant admin moderation. Inappropriate behavior or fake listings result in swift permanent account suspension.

🛡️ Platform Instructions & Community Guidelines

How Giving Tree Works & Our Platform Role

Giving Tree is a peer-to-peer neighborhood bridge connecting donors and receivers directly across Pune.

Direct P2P Platform (Like OLX)

Giving Tree provides the platform and communication tools to connect donors with verified neighbors who need items. We are not a shipping courier or merchant—donors and recipients coordinate handovers directly.

5 Active Requests Quota

To ensure fair community access for all Pune residents, each neighbor may hold a maximum of 5 active requests at a time. Please complete or cancel existing handovers before requesting new items.

10 Pune Neighborhood Hubs

All items are organized around 10 designated local hubs (Kothrud, Baner, FC Road, Hinjawadi, Viman Nagar, etc.). Always arrange meetups at safe public landmarks (metro stations, cafes, main gates).

Community Disclaimer & Reporting

Users are responsible for verifying items at handover. If you encounter any no-show, fake listing, or inappropriate behavior, report it immediately—our team will review and ban violating accounts.
